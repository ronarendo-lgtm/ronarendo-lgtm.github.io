Personal Google Drive integration

Your files. Your Drive. Your control.

Personal Drive Sync is a privately operated setup using rclone, an open-source command-line tool, to access and manage a Google Drive account with permission from its owner.

OAuth 2.0 authorizationUser-controlled operationsNo sign-in on this website

How it works

01

Authorize

The account owner grants the selected Google Drive permissions through Google's OAuth authorization flow, initiated by rclone.

02

Transfer

rclone performs file listing, transfers, synchronization or other actions explicitly requested by the owner, according to granted permissions.

03

Stay in control

Google account access can be revoked at any time in the account's Google security settings.

About this website: This is an informational homepage for a personal rclone Google Drive OAuth client. It is not a login portal and does not receive OAuth callback codes, access tokens or Google Drive files.

Privacy by design

This static website has no forms, analytics scripts or user account system. The separate, locally configured rclone application processes Google Drive information only in response to operations initiated by the user. See the policy for details about permissions, handling of files and tokens, and revoking access.
