Personal Drive Sync

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 11, 2026

Personal Drive Sync is a personal Google Drive integration configured with the open-source rclone tool. This policy describes how this personal configuration may access and handle data.

1. Information accessed

With the account owner's OAuth authorization, rclone may access Drive files, folder structures, file names, metadata, and other information permitted by its selected scopes. Access and refresh tokens are used to authenticate with Google. Depending on the permissions selected, rclone can read, upload, modify, or delete files.

2. Purpose of use

Drive information is processed to carry out actions initiated by the owner, including listing, copying, uploading, downloading, moving, and syncing files. This website does not request Google Drive access.

3. Storage and security

rclone operates on a device or server configured by its owner. Authentication tokens and settings are retained by the rclone configuration unless an alternative is configured. Data transferred or cached by rclone depends on the commands run and configured destinations. The owner should safeguard their rclone configuration, accounts, devices, and copies of files.

4. Sharing and third parties

Data is exchanged with Google to perform authorized Drive operations, and may be sent to other storage locations explicitly configured by the owner. This website does not sell data, run advertising, or contain analytics scripts. GitHub Pages hosts the website and may process standard website request information under its privacy statement.

5. Retention and deletion

Files and local configuration remain until the owner changes or deletes them. The owner can remove rclone configuration, delete transferred copies, or revoke Google authorization. Revoking authorization does not delete copies already transferred. This static website does not maintain a database of Google Drive files or OAuth tokens.

6. Revoking access

The owner can revoke the application's access from Google Account connections. They can also delete or change the rclone remote configuration.

7. Website data

The public website has no forms, login, application cookies, or analytics scripts. GitHub, as its host, may collect ordinary access logs under its own policies.

8. Updates and contact

This policy should be updated if the personal integration's data practices change. For inquiries, contact the owner using the support contact provided on the Google OAuth consent screen or via their GitHub profile.

Note: This policy applies to the owner's personal rclone configuration, not to every rclone deployment. See rclone privacy information and Google's privacy policy.
